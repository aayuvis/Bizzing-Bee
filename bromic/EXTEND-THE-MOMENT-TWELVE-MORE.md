## Trade additions

### B6. The LinkedIn Post
`BOTH` · `ASSET` · `BUILDER` · `FAME` · `R-LO`

> **340 likes. Nobody has stood in it since.**  
> Extend the Moment.

**The scene** — A builder photographs his finished terrace at golden hour for social. It is beautiful and completely empty. Cut to the same terrace in November, still empty.

**Insight** — The finished-project post is the custom builder's single most important marketing act, and it is always shot empty.

**The commercial argument** — An outdoor room that photographs beautifully but cannot be occupied is a portfolio piece that never becomes a testimonial. Heat is what turns the post into a referral.

---

### B7. The Handover Pack
`BOTH` · `ASSET` · `BUILDER` · `CONSIDER` · `R-LO`

> **Appliance warranties. Paint codes. Maintenance schedules. “Terrace — see Spring.”**  
> Extend the Moment.

**The scene** — A builder presents a beautiful leather-bound handover manual. Every system in the house documented. The client turns to the last page.

**Insight** — The handover pack is the last thing a builder controls and the first thing a client judges them by six months later.

**The commercial argument** — Everything else in the house works on the day it is handed over. The terrace is the only room delivered with a season attached — and the only one the builder will hear about again.

out-of-home, because it needs no picture at all.
