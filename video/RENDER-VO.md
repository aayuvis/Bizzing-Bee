# Rendering the episode voiceover

    cd /home/user/Bizzing-Bee/video
    python3 prep.py          # script .md  ->  segs.json  (12 sections)
    python3 render_vo.py     # segs.json   ->  vo/full/*.wav  (Gemini TTS)

**Voice: `Despina`. Model: `gemini-2.5-pro-preview-tts`.** Chosen from a 20-voice audition;
the direction below is byte-identical to the sample it was chosen on, so re-rendering
cannot drift from what was approved.

```
Speak at a natural, brisk conversational pace, like a great storyteller talking to a friend.
Warm, confident, engaging. Keep it moving. Do NOT slow down, do NOT add long dramatic pauses,
do NOT sound solemn or ponderous.
```

## Why the text is preprocessed, not fed in raw

`prep.py` does three things that matter, all of them learned the hard way:

- **Years become words.** `1925` → "nineteen twenty-five", or the model is liable to read it
  as a cardinal number.
- **Spelled-out letters become separate tokens.** `G–L–A–D–I–O–L–U–S` → `G. L. A. D. I. O.
  L. U. S.` Fed raw, the em-dashes are read as pauses inside one mangled word — fatal in a
  film whose whole subject is spelling words out loud.
- **Stage directions and markdown are stripped**, so `*On screen: …*` is never narrated.

## Why it renders per section

Twelve separate files, one per beat marker. A line that comes out wrong is re-rendered on
its own instead of re-recording eight minutes — and the section files are what the editor
cuts against. `--- 0.45s of silence between sections ---` is added at concat so the joins
have air; without it, beats collide.

Output is loudness-normalised to **I=-16 LUFS, TP=-1.5** (streaming-standard), so the VO
sits at a predictable level under music.

## Not in git

`video/vo/*.wav` and the section mp3s are ignored — they are fully regenerated by the two
scripts above, and CLAUDE.md's rule is that a reproducible artifact is gitignored before its
first commit. The full mixed track is kept as the reference render.
