Bizzing Bee — Privacy Policy and Notice to Parents

Effective 9 August 2026 · Written for parents and guardians, under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA, 16 CFR Part 312)

The short version

We do not hold a record of your child. Not their name, not their age, not a photo, a voice recording or a location. A speller profile is a display name they invent and a spelling level — nothing that identifies a person. The database has no column for the rest, so it cannot be collected later by accident.

Their practice — words attempted, scores, streaks, coins — is saved on your device and backed up to your account, so a wiped or lost tablet does not cost them months of work. It is stored against a random id, not a name.

The one piece of personal information we hold is your email address, because somebody has to own the account and be billed. It is an adult's, given by the adult. There is no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third party receives any of it.

There is now a parent account — an email address and a password you choose. It is created and kept on your own device and is not sent to us or to anyone else; we have no server to send it to. Your child never signs in to anything. Please read section 8 before choosing that password.

Bizzing Bee is a spelling-bee training app for children aged roughly 8–15, operated by its developer ("we"). Questions about this policy or your child's information:

2. What information the app handles — and where it lives

When you create a speller profile, the app asks for a first name (or nickname) and an age, and lets your child pick an avatar. As your child plays it also records practice progress: words attempted, scores, levels, coins and settings.

It is stored on your device first, and the app works with no connection at all. Practice is then backed up to your account so it survives a wiped or replaced tablet. What travels is the display name, the spelling level and the progress — against a random id. Your child's real name and age never travel, because the app never asks for them.

Creating a parent account stores your email address with our hosting provider so we can recognise you on any device and attach a paid plan to an adult rather than to a child. It is not sold, not shared, and not used to market to you — there is no mailing list. Your password is handled by the authentication service and we never see it.

What syncs, and what does not. Each speller's display name, spelling level and practice progress are backed up to your account against a random id. Nothing that identifies the child travels with it, because the app does not ask for it and the database cannot hold it.

A nickname works exactly as well as a real name — the name is only used to greet your child on screen.

3. What we never do

  • No collection or transmission of personal information from children
  • No email address, phone number, photo, voice recording or location is ever collected from a child — the parent account is the only login, and children do not have one
  • Nothing you or your child enters is transmitted to us; we operate no server that could receive it
  • No advertising, no behavioural profiling, no analytics or tracking scripts, no social features
  • No disclosure of children's information to third parties — we hold none to disclose
  • No prompts nudging a child to share more information than the activity needs (the only thing ever asked for is a first name or nickname and an age)

4. Web hosting and IP addresses

Like every website, the pages, artwork and audio of Bizzing Bee are delivered by a web host — GitHub Pages, with word-audio files served from GitHub's content servers. To deliver a file, a web host necessarily receives the requesting device's IP address, and may keep it briefly in standard server logs for security and traffic purposes. COPPA treats an IP address as a persistent identifier; it is used here solely to support the internal operations of the service (delivering content and maintaining security), which COPPA permits without parental consent. It is not used to profile, track or contact anyone. GitHub's handling of server logs is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Your rights as a parent

Because everything is on your device, you exercise every COPPA right directly, immediately, and without asking us:

  • Review — open the Parent Zone (Progress → Parent zone, behind your parent PIN) to see everything the app knows: profiles, progress, scores and settings.
  • Delete — remove a speller profile in Settings, or clear the browser's site data for this app. Either erases the information permanently; no copy exists anywhere else.
  • Delete the parent account — signing out and clearing this app's site data in your browser removes the account, its email address and its scrambled password along with everything else. There is no copy on any server for us to delete, and nothing to email us about.
  • Refuse further collection — sign out and the app keeps working on the device alone, with nothing leaving it. Delete the account and the backup goes with it.

6. How long information is kept

Local data is kept on your device only for as long as you keep it: until you delete the profile or clear the browser's storage. The backup is kept for as long as the account exists: delete the account and every speller profile and progress record attached to it is deleted with it, permanently. We keep no separate copy, and there is nothing identifying a child in it to keep.

7. Payments

The app and its opening page describe optional paid plans and show their prices, but no payment is collected inside the app today, no card details are ever requested, and no payment processor is integrated. Choosing a plan today records a preference on your device and charges you nothing.

When payments are switched on, purchases will be a parent-only flow: behind the parent PIN, with any personal or payment information collected from the parent — never the child — and handled by a named payment processor which we will identify here. Card numbers will go to that processor and not to us. This policy and the notices in the app will be updated before the first payment is taken, and the effective date above will move.

8. Security — and an honest warning about the password

The best protection for children's data is not to collect it, and that is the design here. The little that exists lives in your browser's own storage, protected by your device. The optional parent PIN keeps children out of Settings, the Parent Zone and anything marked for grown-ups.

Please do not reuse an important password here. The parent account is stored entirely on your device, and its password is scrambled only enough to keep it from sitting in plain sight — it is not protected to the standard a bank or an email provider uses, because there is no server doing the checking. Anyone with access to your unlocked device could recover it. Choose a password you use nowhere else. When accounts move to a real server, passwords will be properly hashed there, this policy will say so, and the effective date above will move.

For the same reason, the sign-in is a convenience and an ownership record — it is not a vault. Nothing behind it is secret from someone holding your unlocked device.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change in a material way — for example, if accounts or online sync were added — this policy will be updated before the change ships, the effective date above will move, and where COPPA requires it we will provide direct notice and obtain verifiable parental consent before any new collection begins.

10. About Bizzing Bee & sources

Bizzing Bee is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Scripps National Spelling Bee, the North South Foundation, or Merriam-Webster. Those names appear in the app only to describe which competitions the practice material relates to — for example, a list of words that have appeared in past bees.

Definitions, example sentences and hints are written for this app and are not taken from any official dictionary. At a real bee, the official dictionary — Merriam-Webster Unabridged, for Scripps — is the final authority on spellings and meanings.

Word pronunciations are synthesized audio. Typefaces are open-licensed (SIL OFL); lesson narration uses the Apache-licensed Kokoro voice model. Third-party licences are listed in the project's LICENSES-THIRD-PARTY.md.

11. COPPA and where to learn more

COPPA is enforced by the U.S. Federal Trade Commission. The FTC explains parents' rights at ftc.gov — Children's Privacy. If you believe this app has not honoured this policy, please email us first — and you may also contact the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
