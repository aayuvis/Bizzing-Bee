Mercator named it for King Atlas of Mauretania, "erudition, humaneness, and wisdom" (Wikipedia, Mercator; Atlas (mythology)). The map sense became general from the mid-17th century (Wikipedia, Atlas). M-W's "Did you know" gives the other account, the frontispiece picture. The card does not assert it.

**Fix 1 (line, `slotLines.weak.byFamily.vow`).** In app3 `trickAnal`, ie/ei scores 4, and a family wins only above 4. So ie/ei alone never makes a word "vow". Across the 53,511 served words, all 338 vow words have ough/augh or a three-vowel run; receive, believe, friend and weird are all "plain". The line would tell a child whose misses were *thought* and *caught* that they mix up ie and ei.

> You mix up vowel teams, like ough or augh, or three vowels in a row.

Side note for the owner: classic ie/ei misses class "plain" and are not ranked, so they never produce a weak spot.
