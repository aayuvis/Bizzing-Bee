## How a row is judged

- **PASS**: an opened source above states the claim, and the row quotes it.
- **FIX**: the evidence says the line is wrong or claims too much. The row gives the new wording, and it is applied to
  `script.json` with a `changes` entry.
- **UNVERIFIABLE**: no opened source states the claim. This is the honest verdict for "true as far as search can tell,
  page not read". The search column says whether search agrees. **No UNVERIFIABLE row found anything wrong.**
  None needs a change; each still needs its page opened.

## The one fix

**G04a · `visitors[3].card (Echo)`.**
- Was: "In Greek stories, later retold by the Roman poet Ovid, Echo was a mountain nymph who could only repeat the last words she heard."
- Now: "To the ancient Greeks, Echo was a mountain nymph. In the Roman poet Ovid's telling, she could only repeat the last words she heard. Her name is the Greek word for a sound that comes back, and English kept it: echo."
- Why: The opened sources support 'mountain nymph'. The 'last words' detail is Ovid's version, so the card now says so instead of crediting it to Greek stories. Cycle 1 flagged this as optional. The evidence for the attribution is search-level.
- Applied to `script.json` with `changes[cycle 4]`. Britannica's 'Echo' was added to the card's sources. The card
  is 41 words, kid-safe and in the house voice ("To the ancient Greeks, …").
  validate.cjs: FAIL 0.
