The Bizzing Bee Library

Seventeen graphic study books, anime edition — cinematic storyboard openers with the full avatar cast, checkpoint quizzes straight from the Word Map, practice hives with real write-in drills, puzzles made of each chapter's own words, and two collections. The books grow up as the reader does: bright daylight in Vol. 1, letterboxed night cinema by Vol. 12.
