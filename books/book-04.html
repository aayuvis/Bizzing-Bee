Greek Prefixes

anti- / hyper- / hypo- (against / over / under)

The big idea
Greek anti- = against, hyper- = over/excessive, hypo- = under/insufficient. In medicine, knowing the prefix IS knowing the diagnosis. In literature, hyperbole and hypocrisy are built from this same trio.
The pro move
HYPERBOLE
Say it. h-ay-p-UR-b-uh-l-ee Spell it. H · Y · P · E · R · B · O · L · E Say it again. h-ay-p-UR-b-uh-l-ee Syllables. h · y · p · e · r · b · o · l · e Sounds → Letters. [hy=HY] [PER=PER] [buh=BO] [lee=LE] Exception. final -e is NOT silent — it is pronounced /ee/ (Greek!); -bole from ballein = throw; hy-PER-buh-lee Origin. Latin Root. Greek hyper=beyond + ballein=to throw

anti- = Against

Antipathy (anti- + pathos) = feeling against = deep dislike. Antithesis = placed against = direct opposite. Antidote (anti- + didonai = give) = given against a poison. Anticlimactic = against the expected climax = disappointingly flat after a buildup. Antipodal (anti- + pous = foot) = feet opposite = exactly opposite side of the earth.

hyper- = Over/Excessive

Hyperbole (hyper- + ballein = throw) = throwing over = deliberate exaggeration for effect. Hypertrophy (hyper- + trophe = nourishment) = excessive growth. Hyperopia (hyper- + ops = eye) = over-seeing = farsightedness. Hypercritical = excessively critical. Hyperventilate = breathe excessively fast.

hypo- = Under/Insufficient

Hypothesis (hypo- + thesis = placing) = placed under consideration. Hypocrisy (hypo- + krinein = judge) = judging from under a mask = pretending to have values you lack. Hypochondria (hypo- + khondros = cartilage) = below the ribs = once thought the seat of anxiety.

Medical hyper-/hypo- Pairs

Hypertension = blood pressure TOO HIGH. Hypotension = TOO LOW. Hyperthyroid = thyroid overactive. Hypothyroid = underactive. Hyperglycemia = too much blood sugar. Hypoglycemia = too little. Knowing prefix = knowing diagnosis. One pattern unlocks twelve medical terms.

Spelling Traps

Hyperbole ends in -le and the e is PRONOUNCED: hy-PER-buh-lee. Hypocrisy = H-Y-P-O-C-R-I-S-Y (not -acy). Hypothesis → plural hypotheses (Greek -es plural). Antipathy = A-N-T-I-P-A-T-H-Y (-thy not -thee). The -th- = /th/ from Greek theta.

Greek Prefixes

auto- / tele- / micro- / macro- / mega-

The big idea
Five Greek prefixes of size and distance — from "self" to "far," from "tiny" to "enormous." These appear in science, technology, and everyday vocabulary. Learning them unlocks everything from microscope to megalomania.
The pro move
AUTOMATON
Say it. aw-t-AH-m-uh-t-ah-n Spell it. A · U · T · O · M · A · T · O · N Say it again. aw-t-AH-m-uh-t-ah-n Syllables. a · u · t · o · m · a · t · o · n Sounds → Letters. [aw=AU] [TOM=TO] [uh=MA] [ton=TON] Exception. -maton from Greek matos = acting of itself; -on not -an; stress: aw-TOM-uh-ton; plural automatons or automata Origin. Greek Root. Greek automatos = acting of itself

auto- = Self

Automaton (auto- + matos = acting) = acts by itself. Autophagy (auto- + phagein = eat) = a cell digests itself. Autodidact (auto- + didaskein = teach) = self-taught person. Autocracy (auto- + kratos = rule) = rule by one person alone. Autonomy (auto- + nomos = law) = self-governance.

tele- = Far

Telepathy (tele- + pathos) = feeling from far away. Teleology (tele- + logos) = study of purposes/ends. Telekinesis (tele- + kinesis = movement) = moving things from a distance. Telegraph = write from far. Telescope = see far. Telemetry = measure from far (used in spacecraft).

micro- vs macro-

Microcosm (mikros = small + kosmos) = small world. Macrocosm = the large or entire world. Microscope = looks at tiny things. Macroscopic = visible to the naked eye. Microphone = makes small voices large. Macroeconomics = large-scale economics. Small vs large — one prefix difference.

mega- = Very Large

Megalomania (mega- + mania) = delusions of great power. Megalopolis (mega- + polis = city) = a vast metropolitan area. Megalith (mega- + lithos = stone) = a very large stone (like Stonehenge). Megahertz = one million cycles per second. mega- became a general intensifier in everyday language.

Spelling Traps

Automaton = A-U-T-O-M-A-T-O-N (singular); Greek plural = automata. Telepathy ends in -thy not -tee. Megalomaniac = M-E-G-A-L-O-M-A-N-I-A-C. Autodidact = A-U-T-O-D-I-D-A-C-T. Each is built precisely from Greek parts — if you know the roots, you can spell the word.

Greek Prefixes

bio- / geo- / photo- / hydro- / thermo-

The big idea
Five Greek scientific prefixes — life, earth, light, water, heat — forming the backbone of natural science vocabulary. Every science class uses them; every spelling bee tests them. All require ph not f.
The pro move
PHOTOSYNTHESIS
Say it. f-oh-t-oh-s-IH-n-th-uh-s-ih-s Spell it. P · H · O · T · O · S · Y · N · T · H · E · S · I · S Say it again. f-oh-t-oh-s-IH-n-th-uh-s-ih-s Syllables. p · h · o · t · o · s · y · n · t · h · e · s · i · s Sounds → Letters. [foh=PHO] [toh=TO] [SIN=SYN] [thuh=THE] [sis=SIS] Exception. photo- = light; -syn- = together; -thesis = placing; -th- = /th/ (Greek theta); ph not f = Greek signal Origin. Latin Root. Greek photos = light + synthesis = putting together

bio- = Life

Biology = study of life. Biome = a life community. Biopsy (bio- + opsis = seeing) = visual examination of living tissue. Symbiosis (sym- + bio-) = living together mutually. Antibiotic (anti- + bio-) = substance that kills living organisms (bacteria). All life is bio-.

geo- = Earth

Geography = writing about the earth. Geology = study of earth. Geothermal = earth-heat. Geodesic (geo- + daiein = divide) = shortest path on a sphere. Geocentric = earth-centered view. Apogee (apo- + geo-) = point in orbit farthest from earth.

photo- = Light

Photosynthesis = plants make food from light. Photogenic = looks good in light. Photon = a particle of light. Photograph = written by light. Photophobia = sensitivity to light. ⚠️ All photo- words use ph — never f — because this is Greek.

hydro- = Water

Hydrolysis (hydro- + lysis = breaking) = breaking by water. Hydroponics = growing in water. Hydraulic (hydro- + aulos = pipe) = water through pipes. Hydrophobia = fear of water. Dehydrate = de- + hydro- = remove water from.

thermo- = Heat

Thermometer = measures heat. Thermostat (thermo- + statos = standing) = keeps heat steady. Thermodynamics = study of heat and energy. Endothermic (endo- = within) = absorbs heat. Exothermic (exo- = out) = releases heat. Note: geothermal combines both geo- and thermo- in one word.

Greek Prefixes — Body & Mind

neuro- / cardio- / osteo- / psycho- / derm- / hem-

The big idea
Six Greek body-system prefixes that unlock all medical specialist names and condition vocabulary. Know these six and the science section of any spelling bee becomes a pattern-matching exercise.
The pro move
NEUROLOGY
Say it. nuu-RAH-luh-jee Spell it. N · E · U · R · O · L · O · G · Y Say it again. nuu-RAH-luh-jee Syllables. nuh · raa · lah · jhiy Sounds → Letters. [nyoor=NEU] [OL=ROL] [uh=O] [jee=GY] Exception. -neuro- = nerve; -logy = study; -gy = /jee/ at end; eu = /yoor/ diphthong: nyoor-OL-uh-jee Origin. Greek Root. Greek neuron = nerve + logos = study

neuro- = Nerve

Neurology = study of the nervous system. Neuropathy = nerve disease. Neuroticism = anxiety-prone personality trait. Neurosis = a mild mental disorder. Neurotransmitter = chemical messenger between nerve cells. The eu = /yoor/ pronunciation catches many spellers off guard.

cardio- = Heart

Cardiology = study of the heart. Cardiovascular = heart and blood vessels. Electrocardiogram = E-L-E-C-T-R-O-C-A-R-D-I-O-G-R-A-M = record of heart electricity (EKG). Myocardium (myo- = muscle) = the heart muscle. Pericardium (peri- = around) = membrane surrounding the heart.

osteo- = Bone / psycho- = Mind

Osteoporosis (osteo- + poros = passage + -osis) = bones becoming porous and fragile. Osteopathy = treating disease through bone/tissue manipulation. Psychology = study of the mind. Psychosis = a serious mental disorder. Psychosomatic (psycho- + soma = body) = mind-body interaction. ⚠️ Psyche has a SILENT p: P-S-Y-C-H-E.

derm- = Skin

Dermatology = study of skin. Dermatitis = skin inflammation. Epidermis (epi- + derma) = outer skin layer. Hypodermic (hypo- + derm-) = under the skin = type of injection. Taxidermy (taxi- = arrange + derma) = arranging skin = mounting animals.

hem- = Blood

Hematology = study of blood. Hemoglobin = the oxygen-carrying protein in blood. Hemorrhage (hem- + rhegnynai = burst) = blood bursting out. ⚠️ British spelling: haemorrhage — both are correct. Hemophilia (hem- + philia = love) = condition where blood loves to flow (does not clot properly).

Greek Prefixes — Position & Relationship

meta- / para- / epi- / syn- / peri-

The big idea
Five Greek positional prefixes describing relationships between things — appearing in philosophy, science, grammar, medicine, and championship spelling bees. Each has a precise spatial or relational meaning.
The pro move
METAMORPHOSIS
Say it. meh-tuh-MAW-rfuh-suhs Spell it. M · E · T · A · M · O · R · P · H · O · S · I · S Say it again. meh-tuh-MAW-rfuh-suhs Syllables. meh · tah · mao · rfah · sah · s Sounds → Letters. [meh=ME] [tuh=TA] [MOR=MOR] [fuh=PHO] [sis=SIS] Exception. meta- = beyond/change; -morph- = form; -ph- = /f/ (Greek!); -osis = process; stress: meh-tuh-MOR-fuh-sis Origin. Greek Root. Greek meta = change + morphe = form + sis = process

meta- = Beyond / Change

Metamorphosis = change of form beyond what came before. Metaphor (meta- + pherein = carry) = carrying meaning beyond the literal. Metabolism (meta- + ballein = throw) = chemical throwing-around = bodily chemical processes. Metacognition = thinking about your own thinking = thinking beyond ordinary thought.

para- = Beside / Beyond

Parable (para- + ballein = throw beside) = a story placed alongside the point = allegory. Paralysis (para- + lysis = loosening beside function) = inability to move. Paraphernalia = things beside the main items. Paradox (para- + doxa = opinion) = opinion beside expectation = a self-contradicting statement.

epi- = Upon / Over / At

Epiphany (epi- + phainein = show) = a showing upon you = sudden realization. Epithet (epi- + tithenai = place) = placed upon = a descriptive label. Epilogue = words upon the end. Epidemic (epi- + demos = people) = upon the people = spreading disease. Epitaph = words upon a tomb.

syn-/sym- = Together

Synonym (syn- + onoma = name) = same-name word. Synchronize = put in time together. Synthesis = place together = combine. Symphony = sounds together. ⚠️ Sym- before b/m/p: symbol, symmetry, sympathy. Syn- everywhere else: synagogue, syntax, syndrome.

peri- = Around

Periphery (peri- + pherein = carry) = carried around the edge. Perimeter (peri- + metron = measure) = measurement around a shape. Pericardium = membrane around the heart. Perihelion (peri- + helios = sun) = nearest point to the sun in an orbit. Peripatetic (peri- + patein = walk) = traveling from place to place.

The big idea

The pro move

poly- / multi- / omni- / pan- (many / all)

The big idea
Greek poly- = many, Latin multi- = many, Latin omni- = all, Greek pan- = all. Four ways to say "many/all" — each with its own vocabulary territory and spelling patterns.
The pro move
OMNISCIENT
Say it. ah-MNIH-shuhnt Spell it. O · M · N · I · S · C · I · E · N · T Say it again. ah-MNIH-shuhnt Syllables. om · ni · scient Sounds → Letters. [om=OM] [NISH=NI] [ent=SCIENT] Exception. omni- = all; -scient from scire = know; -sc- cluster; -ent not -ant; stress: om-NISH-ent Origin. Latin Root. Latin omnis = all + sciens = knowing

poly- = Many (Greek)

Polygon (poly- + gon = angle) = many-angled figure. Polyglot (poly- + glotta = tongue) = speaks many languages. Polyphony = many voices. Polymath (poly- + manthanein = learn) = one who knows many things. The y in poly- is a Greek vowel signal — not the same as the English word "poly."

multi- = Many (Latin)

pan- = All (Greek)

Pandemic (pan- + demos) = disease affecting all people. Panorama (pan- + horama = view) = all-encompassing view. Panacea (pan- + akos = remedy) = remedy for all ills. Pandemonium (pan- + daimon = spirit) = all the spirits together = utter chaos. Pantheon (pan- + theos = god) = all the gods.

Greek Suffixes

-ism / -ist (doctrine / practitioner)

The big idea
Greek -ismos = doctrine or practice; -istes = practitioner. Every -ism is a belief system or practice; every -ist is the person who holds or practices it. The pair is always structurally parallel.
The pro move
NARCISSISM
Say it. n-AH-r-s-ih-s-ih-z-uh-m Spell it. N · A · R · C · I · S · S · I · S · M Say it again. n-AH-r-s-ih-s-ih-z-uh-m Syllables. n · a · r · c · i · s · s · i · s · m Sounds → Letters. [nar=NAR] [SIS=CIS] [iz=SIS] [um=M] Exception. double-S from Greek name Narcissus; -ism not -izm; stress: NAR-sis-iz-um (4 syllables) Origin. English Root. Greek Narkissos = youth who fell in love with his own reflection

The Pair

-ism = the belief/practice itself. -ist = the person. Nihilism (nihil = nothing) = belief nothing has meaning; nihilist = one who holds this. Narcissism = excessive self-admiration; narcissist = one who is narcissistic. The pair is always perfectly parallel. Know the -ism, you know the -ist.

-ism in Religion & Philosophy

Monotheism (mono- + theos) = one god. Polytheism = many gods. Atheism (a- + theos) = no god. Agnosticism = uncertain about gods. Stoicism = philosophy of endurance. Sophism = clever but misleading argument. Solipsism (solus = alone + ipse = self) = only the self exists.

-ism in Social/Political Terms

Ostracism (ostrakon = clay tablet used for voting out) = exclusion. Schism (skhizein = split) = a major split in a group. ⚠️ Schism = SKIZ-um — the sch = /sk/, making it one of the trickiest spellings in English. Nepotism (nepos = nephew) = favoring relatives. Both are classic bee words.

Literary -isms

Euphemism (eu- + pheme = speech) = a gentler substitute word. Aphorism = a concise, wise saying. Malapropism = a word wrongly used for a similar-sounding one. Anachronism (ana- + chron-) = something out of its time. Pleonasm = using more words than necessary. Catachresis = deliberate misuse of a word for effect.

Spelling Traps

Schism = S-C-H-I-S-M (sch = /sk/; do not write shism or skism). Narcissism = N-A-R-C-I-S-S-I-S-M (double-s from Narcissus). Ostracism = O-S-T-R-A-C-I-S-M. Catechism (kata- + ekhein = sound down) = C-A-T-E-C-H-I-S-M (instruction by question and answer). All have the -ism ending but their roots vary dramatically.

Greek Suffixes

-ology / -ologist / -ographer (study of)

The big idea
Greek logos = word/reason/study. The -ology suffix = the study of. One of the most productive patterns in English — know the root, know the field, know the specialist name.
The pro move
OPHTHALMOLOGY
Say it. ah-p-th-uh-m-AH-l-uh-j-ee Spell it. O · P · H · T · H · A · L · M · O · L · O · G · Y Say it again. ah-p-th-uh-m-AH-l-uh-j-ee Syllables. o · p · h · t · h · a · l · m · o · l · o · g · y Sounds → Letters. [off=OPH] [thal=THAL] [MOL=MOL] [uh=O] [jee=GY] Exception. -ophthalm- from ophthalmos = eye; -ph- = /f/ AND -th- = /th/ both in same word; 5 syllables Origin. Greek Root. Greek ophthalmos = eye + logos = study

The -ology Pattern

Root + -ology = study of that root. Biology = study of life. Geology = earth. Vexillology (vexillum = flag) = flags. Campanology (campana = bell) = bells. Dendrochronology (dendro = tree + chron + -ology) = tree-ring dating. Know any root; add -ology; you have a field of study.

The -ologist Specialist

Add -ist after the -olog-: seismologist (seismos = earthquake). Graphologist = handwriting. Gerontologist (geron = old man) = aging. Ornithologist (ornis = bird) = birds. Entomologist (entomon = insect) = insects. ⚠️ Etymologist (word origins) ≠ entomologist (insects). A classic confusable pair.

Long -ology Championship Words

Dendrochronology = D-E-N-D-R-O-C-H-R-O-N-O-L-O-G-Y. Eschatology (eskhatos = last) = study of the end times. Epistemology (episteme = knowledge) = how we know things. Phenomenology = study of conscious experience. Paleontology = study of fossils. Break each into its roots and they spell themselves.

-ographer = One Who Records

-graphos = one who writes. Choreographer = designs dances. Stenographer = writes in shorthand. Biographer = writes a life story. Lexicographer = writes dictionaries. Oceanographer = records the oceans. Same -graph- root family as -ology — just focused on writing/recording rather than studying.

Etymology vs Entomology

Etymology = etymon (true form) + -logy = study of true word forms — E-T-Y-M-O-L-O-G-Y. Entomology = entomon (cut in pieces = insect's segmented body) + -logy — E-N-T-O-M-O-L-O-G-Y. The second letter is the tell: ety- vs ento-. These are the most confused -ology words in spelling bees.

Greek Medical Suffixes

Medical Suffixes: -itis / -osis / -ectomy / -plasty / -scope / -meter

The big idea
Six Greek medical suffixes that together explain the structure of nearly every medical and surgical term. Know these six patterns and you can decode medical vocabulary on sight — even words you have never seen before.
The pro move
APPENDECTOMY
Say it. a-pih-NDEH-ktuh-mee Spell it. A · P · P · E · N · D · E · C · T · O · M · Y Say it again. a-pih-NDEH-ktuh-mee Syllables. ae · pih · ndeh · ktah · miy Sounds → Letters. [uh=AP] [pen=PEN] [DEK=DEC] [tuh=TO] [mee=MY] Exception. -ectomy = surgical removal; appendix + -ectomy; -ec- not -ik-; stress on third syllable uh-pen-DEK-tuh-mee Origin. Latin Root. Latin appendere = to hang + Greek ektome = excision, from ek- = out +

-itis = Inflammation

Any -itis word = inflammation of [root]. Laryngitis (larynx) = inflamed voice box. Gastritis (gaster) = inflamed stomach. Gingivitis (gingiva = gum) = inflamed gums. Appendicitis = inflamed appendix. The root tells you WHERE; -itis tells you WHAT is happening. Simple, reliable, powerful.

-osis = Condition or Process

Cirrhosis (kirrhos = yellow-orange) = liver scarring. Osmosis = passage through a membrane. Thrombosis = blood clot. Fibrosis = excess fibrous tissue. Diagnosis (dia- + gnosis) = knowing by analysis. Prognosis (pro- + gnosis) = knowing in advance. -osis = an ongoing condition or biological process.

-ectomy and -otomy

-ectomy (ek- = out + tome = cutting) = surgical REMOVAL: tonsillectomy, appendectomy, vasectomy, mastectomy. -otomy/-tomy = cutting INTO (but not necessarily removing): dichotomy (dikho- = in two), lobotomy, tracheotomy. Key: -ectomy = remove; -tomy = cut open.

-scope and -meter

-skopein = to look. Kaleidoscope (kalos = beautiful + eidos = form), periscope (peri- = around), stethoscope (stethos = chest), endoscope (endo- = within). -metron = measure. Altimeter (height), spectrometer (spectrum), odometer (distance), barometer (pressure).

-plasty = Surgical Repair

-plastos = molded. Rhinoplasty (rhino- = nose) = nose surgery. Angioplasty (angio- = vessel) = vessel repair. Arthroplasty (arthro- = joint) = joint replacement. Otoplasty (oto- = ear) = ear surgery. The -plasty suffix always means molding or repairing tissue surgically.
