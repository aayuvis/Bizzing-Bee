The big idea
The most common suffix in English — from Latin -tionem. Three spellings, one /shun/ sound, thousands of words. The right spelling depends on the root that precedes it.
The pro move
EXACERBATION
Say it. ih-gza-ser-BAY-shuhn
Spell it. E · X · A · C · E · R · B · A · T · I · O · N
Say it again. ih-gza-ser-BAY-shuhn
Syllables. ih · gzae · ser · bey · shah · n
Sounds → Letters. [eg=EX] [ZAS=AC] [er=ER] [BAY=BA] [shun=TION]
Exception. -tion = /shun/ always; from exacerbare = make harsh; ex- prefix; stress: eg-ZAS-er-BAY-shun
Origin. Latin
Root. Latin acerbus = harsh, bitter

The Three Spellings

-tion = after most roots: exacerbation, perdition, scintillation. -sion = after d, l, n, r + vowel: extension, mansion, revision. -ssion = after stressed vowel: mission, passion, session, permission. -cion = rare: coercion, suspicion.

Does the root end in d or se? Use -sion: extend → extension, revise → revision. Does it end in t or te? Use -tion: act → action, create → creation. Does it end in ess? Use -ssion: express → expression, suppress → suppression. The root ending i…

The big idea
The -ous adjective family means "full of" or "having the quality of." Choosing -ious, -eous, or -uous depends on the noun base. These endings produce some of the richest vocabulary in English.
The pro move
PERSPICACIOUS
Say it. per-spuh-KAY-shuhs
Spell it. P · E · R · S · P · I · C · A · C · I · O · U · S
Say it again. per-spuh-KAY-shuhs
Syllables. per · spi · ca · cious
Sounds → Letters. [per=PER] [spih=SPI] [KAY=CA] [shus=CIOUS]
Exception. -acious from Latin -ax stem; noun form = perspicACITY (same -cac- spelling, different stress)
Origin. Latin
Root. Latin perspicere = to see clearly, from per = through + specere = to l

Chapter 3 · -able / -ible (capable of being)The idea · ★★☆
The big idea
Two spellings, one /uh-bul/ sound. The right choice depends on whether the root can stand alone as an English word. Championship spellers know both the rule and the exceptions.
The pro move
INDELIBLE
Say it. ih-NDEH-lih-buhl
Spell it. I · N · D · E · L · I · B · L · E
Say it again. ih-NDEH-lih-buhl
Syllables. ih · ndeh · lih · bah · l
Sounds → Letters. [in=IN] [DEL=DEL] [uh=I] [bul=BLE]
Exception. -ible not -able because root dele- cannot stand alone as an English word; in- = not; stress: in-DEL-uh-bul
Origin. Latin
Root. Latin indelebilis = indestructible, from in+delere = to destroy/erase

Use -able when the root is a complete English word: comfort → comfortable, depend → dependable, break → breakable. Use -ible when the root is a Latin stem that cannot stand alone: aud- → audible, leg- → legible, vis- → visible, del- → indelibl…
