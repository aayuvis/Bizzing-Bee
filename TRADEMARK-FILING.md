# BIZZING — USPTO filing worksheet

Everything needed to complete a `BIZZING` trademark application in one sitting at
**trademarkcenter.uspto.gov**. Fees change; verify current amounts on uspto.gov before paying.

This is a preparation document, not legal advice.

---

## 0. Four decisions to make before you open the form

**1. Who owns the mark — you or an LLC?**
No LLC yet → file as an individual. The priority date is what matters, and assigning a mark
to a company later is routine paperwork. If the LLC is weeks away, waiting is marginally
cleaner. **Do not delay the filing by more than a month for this.**

**2. Which address goes on it?**
Your **domicile address becomes public record**. If you file as an individual, that is your
home address, permanently searchable. This is the strongest practical argument for forming
the LLC first and using a business or registered-agent address. Decide deliberately.

**3. Which email?**
The correspondence email is also public and *will* be scraped. Use a dedicated address on
the domain you now own — `tm@bizzing.app` — never your personal Gmail.

**4. Which classes?** See §3. Recommendation: **9 + 41 + 42**.

Statement of Use or must be deleted.

## 4. Goods and services — copy-paste

Search each phrase in the **ID Manual** inside Trademark Center and select the pre-approved
entry that matches. Using pre-approved wording avoids a per-class surcharge and reduces
examiner objections. Only free-type if nothing fits.

**Class 9**
```
Downloadable computer software for children's spelling, vocabulary and literacy education;
Downloadable mobile applications for children's spelling and vocabulary practice
```

**Class 41**
```
Educational services, namely, conducting classes and workshops in the field of spelling and
vocabulary for children, and distribution of course materials in connection therewith;
Arranging and conducting spelling bee competitions;
Providing online non-downloadable educational materials in the field of spelling, vocabulary
and etymology for children
```

**Class 42**
```
Providing temporary use of online non-downloadable software for children's spelling,
vocabulary and literacy education
```

**Why worded this narrowly:** every entry names *children*, *spelling* and *education*.
Generic wording like "downloadable mobile applications" is the exact phrase used by serial
99076246 and invites a needless comparison. Specificity is both cheaper and safer.

## 7. Filing sequence

1. **`account.uspto.gov`** — create account, complete **ID.me verification**. Can take a
   day or two; do it first so it is not the blocker.
2. **`tmsearch.uspto.gov`** — re-run `BIZZING` immediately before filing to confirm nothing
   new landed.
3. **`trademarkcenter.uspto.gov`** — file, using §2–§5 above. (Trademark Center replaced
   TEAS; ignore any guide mentioning "TEAS Plus" or "TEAS Standard".)
4. Pay. Record the **serial number**.
5. Track at **`tsdr.uspto.gov`**. Also docket serial **99076246** to watch its fate.

**Timeline:** roughly 8–14 months to registration if unopposed. First examiner action
typically ~6–8 months.

## 8. After filing

- **Office action responses are due within 3 months**, extendable once for a fee. Missing
  the deadline abandons the application.
- **Start using `Bizzing™` immediately** — site, app, books, packaging. No registration
  needed for `™`; it asserts common-law rights and establishes a public use date.
  `®` only after registration issues.
- **⚠️ Scam mail.** Once filed, your name and address are public and you will receive
  official-looking invoices from private companies with governmental-sounding names
  ("Trademark Compliance Office", "Patent & Trademark Bureau", WIPO lookalikes) demanding
  a few hundred dollars. **The USPTO never invoices by postal mail.** Every legitimate fee
  is paid by you, on the site, at filing. Bin anything that arrives in an envelope.

## 9. India — Madrid Protocol

Do **not** file separately at IP India. Within **6 months** of the US filing, file an
international application through the same USPTO account designating India, claiming the US
priority date. Cost: ~$100/class USPTO certification plus WIPO fees
(calculator at `madrid.wipo.int`). Same route later adds UK, EU, Canada, Singapore.

**Caveat — central attack.** A Madrid registration depends on the US base application for
5 years. If the US application dies in that window, the international registrations fall
with it. Given the phonetic overlap with BUZZING, that risk is not zero. If you would rather
decouple them, file India directly instead (TM-A, ₹4,500/class as an individual or
recognised startup) and accept the extra paperwork.
