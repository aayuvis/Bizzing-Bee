The Clarity Hierarchy

Level 1 — Strategic clarity

Does every person know what the organization is trying to achieve?

Level 2 — Consequence clarity

Does every person know what happens if standards are not met?

Level 3 — Standard clarity

Could every person self-assess accurately?

Level 4 — Role clarity

Does every person know exactly what they own?
